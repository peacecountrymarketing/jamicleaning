# Jami Cleaning

Create a third distinct website concept for JAMI Cleaning Inc, a cleaning company in Grande Prairie, Alberta. This should be Option C, clearly different from the first clean blue local-service concept and the second warm premium/local-family-business concept. Make this one bold, polished, confident, and sales-forward while still feeling trustworthy and local.

Business context:
- Company name: JAMI Cleaning Inc
- Location: Grande Prairie, Alberta
- Services: residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, vehicle detailing, yard cleaning/maintenance, lawn services, snow removal, garbage cleanup
- Contact details: Primary phone 780-897-3077, secondary phone 780-897-7444, email Accounting@jamicleaninginc.com
- Social handle: @jamicleaninginc
- Trust points: licensed, bonded and insured; trained reliable staff; high standards; zero cancellation fees; re-cleaning guarantee within 24 hours if dissatisfied; owner/operator certifications include WHMIS, Personal Protective Equipment, Bloodborne Pathogens, Ladder Safety, Pandemic Awareness; 10+ years in cleaning industry; locally based in Grande Prairie.
- Owners: Ramin Jami and Leila Jami

Creative direction for Option C:
- Bold, premium, high-converting service-company look.
- Design should feel energetic and professional, like a business ready for residential and commercial growth.
- Palette: crisp white, deep navy, bright electric blue, charcoal, and small accents of clean aqua. Avoid warm cream/soft family look from Option B.
- Use strong contrast, clean geometric sections, sharp service cards, and confident CTA areas.
- Avoid clutter, glossy old-style buttons, and text embedded in images.
- Prioritize a clear sales path: service need -> trust proof -> quote request.

Primary goal:
Create a website concept that makes JAMI Cleaning look established, reliable, and easy to hire. It should help visitors quickly choose a service, trust the company, and request a quote or call.

Page structure:
1. Top announcement/contact bar
- “Serving Grande Prairie homes, businesses, vehicles & yards”
- Phone: 780-897-3077
- Email: Accounting@jamicleaninginc.com

2. Header
- JAMI Cleaning Inc logo/name
- Nav: Services, Process, Guarantee, About, Contact
- CTA: Request a Quote

3. Hero section
- H1: Professional Cleaning & Maintenance Services in Grande Prairie
- Subheading: From homes and offices to vehicles and yards, JAMI Cleaning Inc helps keep your space clean, cared for, and ready for everyday life.
- CTAs: Request a Quote, Call 780-897-3077
- Include a right-side “instant trust” panel with: Licensed/Bonded/Insured, Re-Cleaning Guarantee, 10+ Years Experience, Locally Owned.
- Visual: professional cleaning or service collage, with clean overlays and readable text.

4. Service pathway section
Title: Choose the service you need
Create four bold cards with icons or imagery:
- Residential Cleaning
- Commercial Cleaning
- Vehicle Detailing
- RV Cleaning
- Aviation Cleaning
- Lawn Care
- Pressure Washing
- Carpet Spot Cleaning
- Specialty Cleaning (Hat & Shoe Cleaning)
- Snow Removal & Garbage Cleanup
Each card should have 3 included-items bullets and a CTA.

5. “How it works” section
Three steps:
- Tell us what needs cleaning
- Get a quote and schedule
- Enjoy a cleaner space
This should make the process feel easy and reduce hesitation.

6. Trust and guarantee section
Title: Reliable service, backed by standards
Include:
- Trained staff
- Licensed, bonded and insured
- Re-cleaning guarantee within 24 hours
- Competitive rates and price matching
- Zero cancellation fees if accurate, worded carefully: “Ask about our zero cancellation fee policy.”

7. Commercial/residential split section
A visually strong two-column section:
- For homes: detailed cleaning, kitchens, bathrooms, floors, recurring or one-time support.
- For businesses: offices, professional presentation, dependable scheduling, sanitation-focused work.

8. Vehicle and yard add-on section
Promote them as extra convenience services:
- Vehicle detailing: interior/exterior, mats, windows, vacuuming, deodorizing, clear coat protectant; note larger vehicles may cost more.
- Yard maintenance: mowing, edging, trimming, lawn aeration, dethatching, weed removal, snow removal, garbage cleanup.

9. About section
Short, confident local-owner copy about Ramin Jami and Leila Jami, owner/operators committed to ongoing education, certifications, and high service standards in Grande Prairie.

10. Review/social proof section
Use polished placeholder testimonials with clear placeholder names or initials. Add CTA to leave/read Google reviews.

11. Final conversion section
Strong CTA block:
- Heading: Ready to get your space handled?
- Short reassurance copy: Send a few details and we’ll follow up with availability and next steps.
- Form layout: name, phone/email, service type dropdown, preferred timing, message.
- Buttons/links: Call primary, call secondary, email.
- Hours: Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM-8:00 PM
- Location: Grande Prairie, Alberta

SEO and accessibility:
- Exactly one H1.
- Strong H2 section headings.
- Meta title: JAMI Cleaning Inc | Professional Cleaning in Grande Prairie
- Meta description: Professional residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, vehicle detailing, and yard maintenance in Grande Prairie from JAMI Cleaning Inc.
- Clickable tel: links and mailto link.
- Accessible contrast, keyboard-friendly nav, mobile-first layout, no horizontal overflow.
- Alt text for all meaningful images.

Tone:
Confident, clean, professional, direct, and conversion-focused. Still friendly enough for a local business. Avoid generic filler and avoid sounding like a huge impersonal franchise.

Deliverable:
A complete Lovable website preview that is intentionally different from Options A and B and ready for Maggie to present as “Option C.”
